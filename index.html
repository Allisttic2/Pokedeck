About Pokedeck

Pokedeck is a free tracker for your Pokémon collection. Pick a game, tap a Pokémon to mark it as caught, and tap the star on a card to mark it as shiny.

How it works

  • Every game has its own list, in that game's regional Pokédex order. The "All Pokémon (National)" view combines everything you have caught in any game. Regional forms (Alolan, Galarian, Hisuian, Paldean) are listed in the games where they appear, and at the end of the National list.
  • Pokémon that cannot be shiny in a game have no shiny star.
  • Your progress is saved only in this browser on this device. It is not uploaded anywhere, and clearing your browser data will erase it.
  • Want it on your home screen? Open the app version, which can be installed and works offline.
  • Download the desktop app for Windows, Mac or Linux (free).
  • There are no accounts, ads or analytics. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and images and data from PokeAPI and GitHub, so those services can see your IP address like any website you visit.
